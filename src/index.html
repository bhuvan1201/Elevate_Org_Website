Quick facts

Vaping is the most common form of nicotine use among U.S. teens. Education and peer-driven programs reduce experimentation and dependence.

Our Mission

We empower students, parents, and educators with evidence-based information and peer-led programming to reduce teen vaping and its long-term harms.

School Workshops

Interactive workshops designed for high school audiences — science of nicotine, social pressures, and how to resist them.

Peer Ambassador Program

Train student ambassadors to lead conversations and create a culture where vaping is not the norm.

Parent & Educator Resources

Toolkits, discussion guides, and tips for spotting signs of vaping and talking to teens about nicotine.
